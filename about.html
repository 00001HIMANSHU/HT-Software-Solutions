Who We Are

A Trusted Software Development Company

HT Software Solutions is an innovative technology company focused on designing and developing websites, mobile applications, AI-powered products, and enterprise software solutions.

Our goal is to deliver high-quality digital products that help businesses automate processes, improve customer experiences, and accelerate growth.

We combine creativity, technology, and business strategy to provide reliable software solutions for clients across different industries.

Founder

Himanshu Tonger

HT Software Solutions was founded by Himanshu Tonger, a Computer Science Engineer passionate about Artificial Intelligence, Software Development, Web Technologies, and Digital Innovation.

During his academic journey, he developed several software projects, including the Resourceful Pedagogy System (RPS), an AI-powered educational platform designed to improve learning experiences.

His vision is to build a technology company that creates innovative software solutions capable of solving real-world problems while delivering exceptional value to clients around the globe.
